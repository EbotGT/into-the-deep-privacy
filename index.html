01 / LOCAL PLAY

Start playing without signing in.

Into The Deep can be played and saved locally without an account.

Your voyages, local backups and archives, preferences, character customization and saved item-grip poses are stored on your device. Local play does not require uploads. The optional cloud controls described below determine whether a saved-voyage copy is sent or a private co-op session is started.

Signing out or deleting an online account leaves your offline files in place. Remove unwanted local copies separately.

02 / OPTIONAL CLOUD PROGRESS

Choose when a voyage is transferred.

Cloud accounts are optional. Signing in checks your cloud status; it does not automatically upload or replace your voyage. Cloud progress uses manual, confirmed copies. These account transfers are separate from the optional iCloud feature below. Private co-op is a separate, explicit create/join action and is never started just by signing in.

Account details
Your chosen username and password are sent over HTTPS to the Supabase-backed account service for registration or authentication. An account identifier links your cloud progress to your account. The game does not write your typed password to preferences or voyage files; session credentials stay in running-process memory. Username accounts have no email password recovery.
Confirmed upload
Only a confirmed upload sends a saved-voyage copy. It includes your game-world position, inventory, survival status, camp and world changes, and discoveries. Game-world position is not your real geographic location. This solo-voyage upload does not include character appearance or saved item-grip poses.
Confirmed download
A download needs separate confirmation and can replace the current local voyage after its appropriate backup or archive. Review the transfer before continuing.

Optional private co-op

Creating or joining a private two-person room sends your game account identifier, chosen display name, character appearance, in-game position and actions, inventory and survival needs through the Supabase-backed service. The other room member receives the shared game state and your character’s appearance and actions. These positions describe the game world, not your real geographic location. Camera images, microphone audio and saved custom grip presets are not sent.

The host’s account keeps the shared island checkpoint, including each survivor’s separate supplies and the shared camps, resources, wildlife and boats. The game also keeps an account/room-scoped local copy. Shared progress never replaces the ordinary solo voyage. Private invitation codes should be shared only with your intended co-op partner. Rooms support two members and expire after 24 hours; closing or expiring a room ends its live access but does not promise immediate deletion of provider logs or backups. The host must stay connected for play to continue.

Optional iCloud save copies

iCloud saves are off until you enable them in iCloud saves. When enabled, successful local saves can publish immutable voyage copies to the game’s private iCloud Documents container under your current Apple account. No separate game account is required. The copies include game-world position, inventory, survival needs, camp, resources and discoveries; appearance, grip presets and passwords are not included.

The game lists up to 48 recent copies. Select and review a copy, then explicitly confirm restoring it; receiving a copy never automatically replaces the active voyage. Before restoration, the game preserves the current local save and backup as separate local archives. Apple delivery may be delayed or unavailable. A locally accepted copy does not prove that another device has received it.

Disabling iCloud saves stops future queued copies but does not remove copies already written, including a write already in progress. The game does not automatically delete older iCloud copies. iCloud storage, account access and provider retention follow your Apple account settings and Apple’s applicable policies. Deleting the separate Supabase game account does not delete iCloud or local files.

03 / ACCOUNT DELETION

Remove your online account in the game.

  1. Open Cloud voyage from the title or pause menu and sign in.
  2. Choose Delete account and read the permanent-deletion warning.
  3. Enter your current password and acknowledge the warning.
  4. Select Permanently delete account to submit the request.

An active offline voyage must save successfully before the request is sent. Backing out before submission does not delete the account.

Removed after success

Online account and progress

Successful deletion removes your online authentication account, profile, solo cloud voyage and every shared room or checkpoint involving your account as host or current or former participant. Those shared sessions end, and the checkpoint is removed as a whole. Your username may become available again.

Kept separately

Local copies and other accounts

Your offline voyages, backups, archives, appearance, grip presets and settings remain on your device. Other players’ independent accounts, solo cloud voyages and unrelated rooms remain. The shared-record deletion protection does not mean playable co-op is available.

If the connection is interrupted

A timeout or cancellation after submission can leave the result unknown, even if the server completed deletion. The game clears its session and explains the uncertainty. If the account still exists, sign in again and review deletion; the game does not automatically recreate it. If session revocation succeeds but deletion fails, the account data remains and you may need to sign in again.

The service reauthenticates the account and revokes refresh sessions before hard deletion. Previously issued access tokens do not become instantly invalid. Current cloud and room queries reject a deleted account’s missing profile or membership, but an already authorized Realtime connection may retain cached permissions until refresh or expiry. Instant connection eviction is not promised.

04 / CAMERA & AUDIO

Manual poses. Supplied voices.

Grip pose studio

The Mac grip studio uses manual hand and item sliders. These controls do not access your camera. Live camera hand tracking is not available in this preview.

In-game voice clips

The game plays supplied offline voice clips. It does not record your microphone for those voices, send player audio to ElevenLabs or generate those voices online during play.

05 / SECURITY & RETENTION

What deletion does—and what can remain.

Abuse prevention

To limit abusive account and deletion requests, the service stores HMAC-derived account and IP buckets with attempt counts and window times. This table does not store raw IP addresses or passwords. These records are pseudonymous, rather than irreversibly anonymous, and can remain after account deletion.

Rows older than two days are removed when a subsequent account attempt runs cleanup. This is lazy cleanup, so it does not guarantee removal within 48 hours.

Provider processing

Supabase and network infrastructure may process operational and authentication logs and backups under their applicable settings and policies. Their precise retention and backup-removal schedules have not been established for this notice. Successful account deletion removes the live application and authentication records described above; it does not promise that every provider log or backup disappears immediately.

Game services

The current game configuration disables Unity runtime statistics submission, Analytics, Engine Diagnostics and other Unity services. This configuration does not imply that the cloud or network infrastructure processes no operational information.

This support website

This privacy and support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including visits made without signing in; see the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Local copies remain until you remove them. Deleting an online account does not erase copies stored on another device.
